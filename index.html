How I work
Step 01

Brief & References

We go over the project, the references and what the images need to communicate. I tell you upfront what I need from your side: plans, materials, brand assets.

Step 02

Blocking & Cameras

Grey-model blocking with camera angles proposed. You approve composition and framing before any material or lighting work starts.

Step 03

Light & Material

Lighting setup, material work and scene population. You get low-resolution previews to review before the final render goes to the farm.

Step 04

Render & Delivery

Final render, compositing and colour. Delivered at agreed resolution with the source files if the project includes them.
